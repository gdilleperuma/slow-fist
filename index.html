How to play

Two stick fighters duel in slow motion. It is not turn based: you read what the opponent is doing, plan a counter, and the physics (reach, angle, speed, balance, stamina) decides the result. Reduce the other fighter's health to zero for a K.O.

Recording and replay

Press V (or the Record button) to start a new round and record it; press V again to stop. Recording also stops by itself two seconds after a K.O. so the fall is included. Press B (or Replay) to watch the last recording at normal speed, whatever the live slow-motion setting is; [ ] change the replay speed from 0.1 to 2.0 and B leaves the replay. The reset, mode and style keys are ignored during a replay.

Save recording downloads a small JSON file (seed, styles, and the inputs of each human player). Load recording reads one back and checks it: the game replays it in the background and rejects a file that does not reproduce exactly (for example one edited by hand, or made by a different version or browser). Export video replays the recording at normal speed and saves it as a .webm video where the browser supports it.

Striking

Hold punch or kick to wind up and release to throw. How long you held picks the move, and the aim angle picks where it lands (high is the head, level is the body, low is the legs). Tap the same key while the strike is in flight to speed it up, up to three times as fast; each tap costs stamina and there is a short gap between taps. Hold time and taps set the speed, and speed sets the power.

Defence

Block: hold block and aim at the incoming strike. In the arc is a block (small damage), near the edge is partial, outside is a hit. Parry: raise the guard just before impact (within a quarter of a second) at the right angle: no damage, the attacker staggers and you get a counter window (your next strike is faster, harder and harder to guard). Guard health drains as you block; at zero the guard breaks.

Dash through an incoming strike to dodge it: you cannot be hit for the first moments, you get a counter window and a little stamina back.

Grapple: close in and grab. It beats a guard, but a crouching, dashing or quickly retreating opponent slips out, it needs a short wind-up, and it has a cooldown. A successful grab flips the opponent over your head for big damage.

Movement

Hold a direction to build speed. Sprint: tap the same direction twice quickly for a burst of extra speed (costs a little stamina). Jump: the fighter crouches for a moment, then springs; hold the key to jump higher. Crouch lowers you, shortens your strides and ducks high strikes.

Hits and knockdowns

Head hits do more damage and leave you dizzy. Body hits drain stamina and double you over. Leg hits slow you, and if you were running you trip. Hits landed while the opponent is still reeling chain into a combo, adding damage for each hit. A hit that is big enough for the target's balance knocks them down; they get up in a sequence (hands and knees, one knee, rise) and cannot be hit during the first moments of it.
