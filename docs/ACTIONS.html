Slow Fist: everything a fighter can do

Each action is shown in three poses taken from the game itself (start, contact, end). Frames are startup / active / recovery at 60 per second. Keyboard keys are for player 1. In the pictures: the yellow dotted line is the path of the fist or foot, the red ring is where it makes contact, the grey dashed line is the angle of the strike, and a squeezed figure is a spin.

Movement

Stand and walk

The stance. Walk toward or away from the opponent.
Keyboard: A / D   Touch: move circle: left / right

Jump

A short hop (tap) or a higher jump (hold). It costs stamina.
Keyboard: W   Touch: move circle: up

Crouch

Ducks under high strikes and slips grabs.
Keyboard: S   Touch: move circle: down

Sprint

Press a direction twice quickly. Faster, but it costs stamina.
Keyboard: tap D twice   Touch: flick the move circle twice

Dash

A quick burst that cannot be hit for a moment: dash through a strike to dodge it.
Keyboard: C   Touch: flick the move circle sideways and let go

Defence

Block

Aim the guard at the incoming strike. It wears down the guard bar.
Keyboard: H   Touch: BLOCK button (or hold the stick away, if switched on)

Parry

Raise the guard just before the hit lands, aimed at it: no damage and a counter chance.
Keyboard: H, just before the hit   Touch: tap BLOCK just before the hit

Grappling

Grapple

Close in and grab. It beats a guard, but a crouching or retreating opponent slips out.
Keyboard: Z   Touch: draw straight back in a circle

Throw

A successful grab becomes a flip over the shoulder. The victim lands protected.
Keyboard: Z, close up   Touch: draw straight back, close up

Special

Boost

For 4 seconds your hits deal 1.5 times the damage. It costs 30 stamina; cooldown 12 seconds.
Keyboard: X   Touch: BOOST button

Turn around

Flips the way you face and keeps it for 3 seconds; you cannot hit what is behind you.
Keyboard: U   Touch: TURN button

After a fall

Knockdown

A heavy hit or a throw puts a fighter on the floor.

Stand up by itself

With no choice, the fighter gets up by itself after the fall (standard, kip-up or tornado).

Kip-up

The fastest way up (0.5 s) but it costs 25 stamina and leaves you open for the last 0.3 s.
Keyboard: W while down   Touch: move circle up while down

Forward roll to stand

Rolls over your shoulder toward the opponent and comes up facing him (0.8 s, 15 stamina, 70 px).
Keyboard: D while down (facing right)   Touch: move circle toward the opponent while down

Back roll away

Rolls backward and away (0.8 s, 15 stamina, 90 px); safe for the first half second.
Keyboard: A while down (facing right)   Touch: move circle away from the opponent while down

Stay down and guard

The slow, safe stand-up (1.4 s, free): protected most of the way, and it rests stamina at the full rate.
Keyboard: S while down   Touch: move circle down while down

Break-fall

A tap just as you land (or in the last quarter second of a throw) shortens the fall by a third and softens a throw by 30 percent.
Keyboard: tap any direction as you land   Touch: tap the move circle as you land
